WebApp to share Gossip


Requirements:
* Login with email and magic link
* Create, edit, and delete gossip posts
* Share gossip posts with other users
* View gossip posts shared by other users
* Posts shared with you can be shared with others
* Posts can be commented on by users who have access to them
* Posts can be upvoted and downvoted by users who have access to them
* Posts can be tagged with topics, and users can filter posts by topic


Technical Requirements:
* Frontend: React with Vite and Typescript
* Backend: Rust with Actix-web and Diesel using Postgres
* Database migrations with Diesel
* Dockerfile for both frontend and backend
